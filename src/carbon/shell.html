      This list

      Enter continues lists; Enter on an empty item exits one level. Alt+C checks selected tasks, or unchecks them if all are checked. Ctrl/Cmd+B or I surrounds a selection. Alt+click adds a cursor; Select next selects matching words. Ctrl/Cmd+Z undoes; Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Z redoes. Ctrl/Cmd+S saves. Alt+P toggles preview. In movable lists, Tab indents and Shift+Tab outdents; elsewhere they move focus. Press Escape then Tab or Shift+Tab within two seconds to leave the editor. Ctrl+M (Mac: Alt+Shift+M) toggles persistent Tab focus navigation. Browser Ctrl/Cmd+D and F stay available.
